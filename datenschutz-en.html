2. Data processing in the app

HYDRATE PLUS helps you track daily fluid intake. Drinks, amounts, hydration goals, favorites, settings, and history entries are stored locally on your iPhone. No user account is required. According to the provider, these records are not transmitted to proprietary servers.

3. Drink reminders

If you enable drink reminders and grant permission, HYDRATE PLUS can schedule local notifications on your iPhone. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings at any time. According to the provider, no proprietary notification server is needed.

4. Free trial

To manage the 30-day free trial, the app may store its start date locally in the iOS Keychain. This information is used to manage access.

5. In-app subscriptions

Paid access is provided through Apple’s in-app purchase system (StoreKit). Apple handles payments and subscription management. The app checks subscription status using Apple’s interfaces. We do not receive credit card numbers. Apple’s privacy terms apply to its processing.

6. No proprietary tracking services

According to the provider, HYDRATE PLUS does not use proprietary analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

7. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we process your message and contact information to respond to your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR, depending on the matter. We delete information when it is no longer needed, subject to statutory retention obligations.

8. Your rights

Subject to the GDPR, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. We cannot directly access hydration records stored only on your device.

9. Changes

We update this policy when app features or data processing practices change.
