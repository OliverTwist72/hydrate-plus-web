Subscriptions

Annual subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, go to Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions to review or cancel yours. Canceling prevents the next automatic renewal.

Drink reminders

HYDRATE PLUS can remind you to drink through optional local notifications. If reminders do not appear, check permissions under Settings → Notifications → HydratePlus and the reminder options in the app.
